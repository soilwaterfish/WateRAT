Route correctly

At a confluence, upstream totals are added. At a divergence, totals propagate along every supplied downstream path. A cycle in the supplied network is an error.

Fetch all orders before accumulation. A POD on a first-order reach must contribute downstream. Apply filter_reportable_reaches(minimum_streamorder = 2) only after routing.
The formatter intentionally produces the same all/instream/non-instream and Forest Service/private field names as capture_sites_within(). That is what makes the two methods directly comparable.
