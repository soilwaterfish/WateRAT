--- title: Canonical schema ---

Data contract

One schema for every state

Downstream functions only operate on validated canonical fields. State-specific source columns stop at the adapter boundary.

Uniquerecord_id is non-missing and unique.
Numericmax_flow_cfs is numeric.
Logicalis_instream is TRUE/FALSE.
GroupFields
Identitystate, right_id, site_id, record_id
Source and usestatus, source, beneficial_use, is_instream
Timingdiversion_start, diversion_end
Rate and volumemax_flow_cfs, diversion_rate, diversion_rate_unit, volume, volume_unit
Provenancereport_url and point geometry

Derived review fields

Network preparation may add comid, nldi_error, and fs_intersection. They are audit fields derived after canonical validation; they are not requirements for a new state adapter.

Do not invent conversions. A rate reported in an unsupported unit remains missing from max_flow_cfs until a documented conversion is implemented.
